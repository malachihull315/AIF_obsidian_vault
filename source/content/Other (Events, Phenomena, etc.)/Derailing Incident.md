The Derailing Incident was a series of events occurring in New Atlantis in 2030. These events included terrorist attacks by the Earth First Society on both the city of New Atlantis itself and the Halo Transport Corporation, the revolution of NACORP's ascendant employees against their mistreatment, and the exposure and dissolution of NACORP's Red division.

## The Flash-Line Attack
- Attack on Flash-Line infrastructure, coordinated by the [[Earth First Society]] under [[Sophia Torres]]
- Caused a [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]] train to crash, only the second crash in Flash-Line history
- This is where the name "Derailing Incident" comes from

## Sylvia's Release
- [[Sylvia (Pale Titan)|Sylvia]], the humanoid [[Pale Titan|pale titan]], held in NACORP's secured research facility underneath New Atlantis
- Released by an EFS agent working undercover in NACORP Yellow division
- Rampage caused some casualties; mostly driven by her horrific treatment by the NACORP research team
- Stopped by [[Jaxon James Davis]] (Blue) and [[Samuel Rose]] (Red)
- Rose was ordered not to intervene so he could keep his villain persona, but did anyway to protect Davis
- Left both Davis and Rose even more disillusioned with NACORP

## Raid on the Earth First Society
- Heckler arranged a meeting with [[Basil Bessho]], a leading Green division operative and the most dangerous active ascendant at the time
- Green division raided the EFS base; Torres was killed
- Prevented further danger to the New Atlantis public

## The Revolt
- Afterwards, Heckler convinced Bessho that NACORP could be beaten
- Bessho was the oldest ascendant in NACORP's employ; Heckler finally convinced him to fight for his own rights
- Bessho, Heckler, Rose, Davis and Sylvia, with a small group of rebel Green soldiers, stormed NACORP headquarters
- Negotiated for better treatment at gunpoint
- Waterloo reluctantly gave them full control of Bullet Point and the connected wormhole network

Ascendance is an [[The Otherplane|Otherplane]]-related phenomenon that causes humans (and likely other sentient organisms) to return from death. While it is unknown how exactly this occurs, speculation has long been a topic among blitzologists, and several popular theories have been developed, particularly the Otherplanar Sentience theory.

People who have undergone ascendance, called ascendants, have often been subjects of special treatment, whether that be positive or negative. In some ancient cultures, ascendants were seen as blessed, being related to the divine. In others, they were shunned as sinful beings. In the modern day, anti-ascendant ideologies still exist, but movements seeking to restrict the abuse of ascendants are gaining traction.

## Cause

Proposed by researchers from the New Atlantis University in 2005, the Otherplanar Sentience theory states that small amounts of energy from the Otherplane are responsible for the development of sentient biological life. When a sentient organism dies, the energy from the Otherplane returns to it through a small Tear. (This is also likely the mechanism by which [[Pale Titan|pale titans]] can Tear through the interplanar barrier.) Ascendance, this theory states, occurs when the Tear remains open. This allows excess energy to flood into the body, reawakening the deceased being and creating a stage 1 ascendant.

## Mechanics

Ascendant people can utilise the Otherplanar energy at will, acting as a natural conductor. Many ascendants have historically seen use in war or energy generation, and some have turned to criminal lifestyles to use their talents. Several countries employ ascendants for military or law enforcement purposes, ascendant soldiers often acting as elite shock troops. [[New Atlantis Centre for Otherplane Research and Hazard Prevention]] has had the highest number of contracted ascendants in the world, and dispatches them often to deal with Otherplane-related threats. However, the recent pro-ascendant activism in the wake of the [[Derailing Incident]] has allowed many of these employees to pursue other options.

## Classification

The Richardson Standard Ascendance Scale is a system by which ascendants are classified and ranked, created by [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]] co-founder Thierry Richardson. Non-ascendants are given a rating of 0, and stage 1 ascendants are given a rating of 1.X, X being a number from 1-5. No stage 2 ascendants have been recorded since the introduction of the Richardson Scale, but they would be given a rating of 2. Over time, an ascendant's connection may strengthen, and thus they would be reclassified further up the scale. As a general rule, higher rated ascendants are significantly rarer than lower rated ones.

- 1.1 rated ascendants do not possess any unusual or anomalous properties, and have very weak connections to the Otherplane. Ascendants at this rating are mostly non-threats and cannot conduct Otherplanar energy independently, but are capable of powering some small devices.
- 1.2 rated ascendants do not possess anomalous properties, but have a stronger connection to the Otherplane and can conduct energy through their body with the aid of an apparatus. The form this energy takes differs case by case, and appears to be "filtered" by the user's perception of it.
- Ascendants rated 1.3 are capable of conducting and using Otherplanar energy without the use of a specialised apparatus.
- Ascendants with a rating of 1.4 are capable of using Otherplanar energy to cause unusual effects via minor distortions in spacetime. This could happen intentionally or subconsciously. Some notable examples are [[Jaxon James Davis|Jaxon Davis]]' ability to temporarily place his body into a semi-real state or [[Samuel Rose]]'s ability to collapse himself and reappear at a different location.
- Ascendants given a 1.5 rating are those who are deemed by [[New Atlantis Centre for Otherplane Research and Hazard Prevention|NACORP]] as capable of mass destruction. Ascendants with this rating are strictly monitored, and historically were often imprisoned or executed to avoid disasters.

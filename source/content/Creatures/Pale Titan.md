The pale titan (*Draco vacuus*) is a rarely seen creature seemingly native to the energy seas of the [[The Otherplane|Otherplane]]. Almost nothing is known of its relation to other animals, if it is related, or its anatomy. It appears to subsist purely upon the energy that is common within the Otherplane, although juvenile specimens seemingly must visit the material plane routinely to consume matter, which is theorised to be required for bodily growth. This event is known as a "titanbreak". They are able to do this using a process called "Tearing", where the titan is able to create a localised wormhole-like construct between the material plane and Otherplane. Pale Titans appear to have no upper growth limit, with recorded specimens being as small as 10 metres and the largest reaching nearly 600. As the titan matures, it appears to become more intelligent, and the size of its Tear increases. Older titans have displayed intelligence roughly equivalent to some primates, being able to understand body language and some vocal cues when given sufficient acclimation.

Titan appearances have been recorded for almost all of human history, and were commonly seen as either divine or demonic by ancient societies. Ancient depictions of the pale titan have been found in many places around the world, with the oldest being discovered in northern Africa. More recently, several titans have been captured and used in [[Otherplane Engine|Otherplane engines]], power generation facilities that are able to directly extract the energy from the Otherplane using the pale titan's wormholes. Otherplane engines are incredibly important for modern power generation, providing over 90% of the world's electricity. As their capabilities far exceed any other method of electricity generation, they are able to do this almost entirely unaided. Otherplane engines (and thus, captive titans) exist in the USA, France, China, Nigeria, [[New Atlantis]], the UAE, Germany, and Argentina. The German engine was the first to be created, and was put into active use in 1984.

## Discovery

The pale titan's name was coined by researcher Belle Greenway shortly after the [[Titanbreak of 1982|titanbreak of 1982]], being the first time it was officially scientifically observed. It was named as such due to its size and the usual colour of its exterior. The famous "Broken Lens" photograph was taken during this incident, displaying the pale titan using its Tear to emerge into the material plane.

## Biology

Pale Titans are serpentine creatures, with a long, highly flexible body, and a large head. The head possesses two small eyes located on the front of its face, which seemingly do not grow with age. Like snakes, titans do not possess external ear openings, but do possess some form of hearing. It is theorised that their skin when unarmoured is highly tuned to detect vibrations and waves, giving the titan a high level of awareness to its surroundings. Its body is lined with flexible, tentacle-like appendages along the sides of its body, seemingly used to aid in movement within fluids. The titan's skin is baggy, scaly, and a mottled light grey colour, but turns entirely black when the titan is armoured.

Armouring is a process which the pale titan undergoes when under threat, using its Tear, or otherwise exposed to the Otherplane. It causes the titan's exterior to become significantly tougher and more damage resistant, to the point of being able to safely exist within the Otherplane's energy seas. Armoured titans are discernible based on the jet black colour of their hide compared to the usual pale grey. The limit to the armoured titan's strength, and the mechanism by which they achieve this state, is unknown. The most popular theory proposes that the titan is able to manipulate the alignment of the molecules that make up its exterior, although this has not been proven. Armoured titans have shown immunity or extreme resilience to radiation, superheating, supercooling, blunt impact, sharp implements, concentrated acidic and alkaline substances, electricity, extreme pressure, and vacuum.

The composition of the pale titan's body is almost entirely unknown, as their armour denies all forms of microscopic analysis and attempts to collect samples. It seems to be an unconscious reaction to contact with other objects, as even when sedated the titan retains its ability to defend itself.

Pale titans are exceptionally unintelligent, likely due to the ease of living by subsisting off of Otherplanar energy. Their few forays into the material plane consist mainly of mindlessly devouring whatever's closest and attempting to remove any obstacles that annoy them.

## History & Mythology

The pale titan has historically been seen as a mythical being, commonly associated with destruction or death due to the species' massively destructive tendencies.

The first recorded depiction of a pale titan was found in northern Africa, in the form of an ancient cave painting.

An alternate Earth where the world stayed mostly the same until the late 20th century. Then contact with [[The Otherplane]] (an energy realm next to our universe) began to change everything. People who die can sometimes come back as ascendants, and huge creatures called pale titans can break through into our world. Companies and governments have found ways to put both to use.

New here? Start with the [[OVERVIEW|Overview]] and the [[Timeline]].

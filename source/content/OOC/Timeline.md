```timeline
+ June, 1975
+ [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]] Open For Business
+ In Brazil, Thierry Richardson and Francis Sylvester found the [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo Shipping Company]], using [[Otherplanar Ascendance|ascendants]] to power long-distance ships. The company quickly becomes a success due to the cost-effectiveness of the method.
  
+ March, 1982
+ [[Titanbreak of 1982|Titanbreak In Germany]]
+ The [[Pale Titan|pale titan]] that would later be known as Dedrick enters the material plane. This is the first time a titanbreak has occurred for several decades, and is the first time a titan was captured. Dedrick is held by the German government.
  
+ August, 1984
+ [[Otherplane Engine|The First Engine]]
+ A partnership between Germany and Halo results in the construction of the first [[Otherplane Engine|Otherplane engine]]. This engine is able to produce more than enough electricity for all of Germany, and [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]] is given access for future projects.
  
+ October, 1984
+ [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]] Rebrands
+ The Halo Shipping Company is now the [[Halo Transport Corporation]], and is on track to become the biggest corporation the world has ever seen.
  
+ January, 1987
+ [[Sousa Wormhole Device|Sousa's Wormhole]]
+ A team of researchers discover the ability of [[The Otherplane|Otherplanar]] energy to create wormholes. With the resources of the whole corporation at their disposal, Alice Sousa's team is able to stabilise the effect by the end of the year.
  
+ August, 1988
+ [[Flash-Line|Birth of the Flash-Line]]
+ [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]] begins construction on a wormhole-enabled rail network. An [[New Atlantis|island in the central Atlantic]] is selected as the main hub for the network, and Halo begins moving workers there.
  
+ November, 1991
+ [[New Atlantis|The Central Atlantic Transport Hub]] Is Complete
+ Construction for the centrepiece of the [[Flash-Line]] is done, and expansion begins. More workers relocate to the Hub, along with some important workers from [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]] and its collaborators.
  
+ July, 1999
+ [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]]'s Titan
+ A titanbreak over the Atlantic results in the construction of another [[Otherplane Engine|Engine]], this time belonging to Halo and Halo alone. This enables the upsizing of the [[Flash-Line|network]] to a massive degree.
  
+ December, 2000
+ [[Halo Transport Corporation|Thierry Steps Down]]
+ Paris Charles Harrison, the former CFO of Halo, takes Richardson's place as co-owner of [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]].
  
+ September, 2001
+ Nine. Eleven.
+ The attacks on the world trade center has the world considering alternative options for long-distance transport. For [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]], business is booming.
  
+ May, 2003
+ [[New Atlantis|New Atlantis Rises]]
+ The freshly named [[New Atlantis|New Atlantic Republic]] declares independence as a city-state after the massive boost in traffic in recent years.
  
+ April, 2004
+ [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]]'s [[New Atlantis|New Home]]
+ The [[Halo Transport Corporation]]'s headquarters is now officially based in [[New Atlantis]], enabling it to exercise its immense authority even more efficiently.
  
+ July, 2004
+ [[New Atlantis Centre for Otherplane Research and Hazard Prevention|Founding of NACORP]]
+ The [[New Atlantis Centre for Otherplane Research and Hazard Prevention]] is created. Its purpose is pretty much in the name. Since [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]] and the [[New Atlantis|New Atlantian Government]] are close partners, access to [[The Otherplane|Otherplanar]] energy is cheap and easy.

+ February, 2005
+ [[Halo Transport Corporation|The Rat King Is Crowned]]
+ Francis Sylvester passes away from "heart complications", leaving Harrison as the sole owner of the [[Halo Transport Corporation|company]].

+ November, 2007
+ [[New Atlantis|NA Joins NATO]]
+ [[New Atlantis]] is given an invitation to the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, and is quick to accept it.
  
+ September, 2028
+ Torres' Trainwreck
+ Sophia Torres and the EFS organize a clandestine attack on the Flash-Line, causing a crash and killing 4 people. One of those people was Luna Heckler, who ascended because of this event.
```

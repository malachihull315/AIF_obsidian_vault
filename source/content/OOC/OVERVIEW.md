## Divergence

An important point to note is the reason that the world stayed basically unchanged until the late 20th century. The biggest reason is that while [[Otherplanar Ascendance|ascendance]] and [[Pale Titan|titanbreaks]] occurred, they weren't nearly as frequent. [[The Otherplane]] basically operates like a tray of water, and if holes are poked in it things in the water will flow towards the holes. Essentially, if titanbreaks and ascendances are occurring, the higher the chance that they occur again, as the Tears located on Earth grow more and more common. The acceleration of the population growth in the 19th century also caused the rate of deaths to increase. Thus, the rate of ascendances. Thus, the rate of titanbreaks. Having easier access to ascendants meant companies like [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]] could start using them, and the advancement of science and technology made things like the [[Otherplane Engine|Otherplane engine]] possible. All of this culminated in the major divergences only happening quite recently.

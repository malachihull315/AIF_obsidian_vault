The New Atlantis Centre for Otherplane Research and Hazard Prevention (NACORP) is an agency under the [[New Atlantis]] government, responsible for all matters related to the [[The Otherplane|Otherplane]]. This includes, but is not limited to, management of the nationally owned [[Otherplane Engine|Otherplane engine]], research into the Otherplane and Otherplanar energy, development of technology related to the Otherplane or Otherplanar energy, the cataloguing and supervision of potentially hazardous [[Otherplanar Ascendance|ascendants]], and the restraint and capture of [[Pale Titan|pale titans]] in the event of a titanbreak. Its headquarters are located in a restricted area approximately 1.2 kilometres away from the New Atlantis coastline.

After the [[Derailing Incident]] and subsequent revolution performed by many of its ascendant employees, NACORP's Red and Blue divisions were disbanded, removing much of NACORP's international influence. In the wake of the death of [[Adam Waterloo]] during the raid by former members of Green division, the agency has yet to elect a new director. Despite this, several high-ranking officials within the agency have given promises not to reinstate the Red or Blue divisions, and to discontinue abusive practices against ascendant employees.

## History

NACORP was founded in 2004, a year after the New Atlantic Republic declared independence. Its early conception was due to New Atlantis' preexisting access to the Otherplanar energy produced by [[Halo Transport Corporation|Halo]]'s [[Otherplane Engine|Otherplane engine]]. It has since become a leading authority on matters related to the Otherplane, and is among the world's largest developers of Otherplanar technology.

## Structure and Management

NACORP is split into several divisions, each overseen by a Chief Manager and coded after colours. These divisions are:

* Green division, acting as a military force responsible for security and protection from Otherplane-related threats. After the resignation of Grant Wells, a new Chief Manager is yet to be instated.
* Yellow division, responsible for (often remote) research on Otherplane-related matters. Members of Yellow division have remained as trusted voices in the blitzology field since the division's inception. The current Chief Manager is Kelly Goodman.
* Orange division, a large engineering corps employed by NACORP for the purposes of developing new Otherplanar technology. The current Chief Manager is Jessica Banks.

Former divisions:

* Blue division was an elite group of high-rated ascendants hand-picked for their ability in combat. All members of Blue division acted anonymously under callsigns only, and were given costumes reminiscent of fictional superheroes to bolster public opinion of NACORP. Before its dissolution, it was managed directly by [[Adam Waterloo]].
* Red division was a secret division unknown to the public that underwent black ops for the New Atlantis government. See the [[#Controversy]] section for further elaboration.

## Controversy

Red division was a secret division of the New Atlantis Centre for Otherplane Research and Hazard Prevention that existed from 2014 to 2030. Its existence was kept entirely secret from everyone outside of NACORP's highest-ranking members, and was only revealed to the world after the revolt by its members.

Red division's purpose was to perform international illegal operations that would suit the New Atlantis government. To suit this purpose, all members of Red division were specially selected high-rated ascendants operating under a persona, similar to the Blue division. These personas would be cast as extremists, terrorists, or other criminals, often directly by NACORP, to avoid linking their actions to New Atlantis. NACORP would often arrange scripted engagements between Blue or Green divisions and members of Red division to promote public opinion of Blue and avoid suspicion.

Many members of Red division were unwillingly coerced into cooperation with the agency shortly after their ascendance. Per an interview with [[Luna Heckler]] (a former member of Red division and a prominent ascendant activist), new ascendants that were deemed appropriate for the job were "offered a choice between signing the contract or signing (their) own death warrant". As NACORP's sensors are able to semi-reliably detect the forming of a high-rated ascendant, many members of Red were kidnapped while still recovering from ascendance and declared dead. Much of this is also true of the similarly dissolved Blue division.

Both Red and Blue divisions were based in a secret location in the Arctic known as Bullet Point. This location was accessible only by a private wormhole network and acted as both a home and prison for all high-rated ascendants that NACORP had in its employ. Also located here were any other ascendants that were deemed problematic enough to restrain, and some high-ranking members of Green division to act as security. Bullet Point is still in use by some of the ascendants who were kept there, and allows the group access to the formerly NACORP-controlled private wormhole network. This allows them to continue acting to combat pale titans or other major threats internationally.

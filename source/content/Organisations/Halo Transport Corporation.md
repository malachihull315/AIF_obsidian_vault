The Halo Transport Corporation (trading name Halo) is a [[New Atlantis|New Atlantian]] (formerly Brazilian) multinational technology company and service provider, and is currently the company with the highest net worth and largest user base in the world. Halo's primary business outlet is the operation of its [[Flash-Line]], a series of trains that span over 90% of the world's countries. Due to the unparalleled efficiency and safety offered by the Flash-Line's wormhole system, it has become the de facto method for long-distance travel and shipping. In addition to this, Halo also owns Eden Energy Solutions, a leading company in the development of [[The Otherplane|Otherplanar]] technology, and the operator of one of New Atlantis' [[Otherplane Engine|Otherplane engines]].

## History

The company was established in 1975, beginning as a shipping company operating mostly in the Atlantic Ocean. The company was founded by Thierry Richardson and Francis Sylvester under the name Halo Shipping Company, and began business using [[Otherplanar Ascendance|ascendant]] employees to power long-distance cargo ships. This method proved extremely cost-effective, and given the social stigma against ascendants at the time, was a common career choice. Halo quickly grew into a leading figure in the transport industry, with several other companies attempting to replicate their success.

In 1982, Sylvester suggested that the company divert some of its resources into development of more advanced Otherplane-related technology. In the wake of a [[Pale Titan|pale titan]] being captured for the first time in Germany, researchers were inspired to design the [[Otherplane Engine|Otherplane engine]], which they presented to the German government. The collaboration quickly bore fruit, and Halo was given access to the first Otherplane engine shortly after its launch in 1984. At this point, shares of the company were valued at nearly US $38 each. Sylvester and Richardson still retained total control. Later this year, the company would be renamed to the Halo Transport Corporation.

With the backing of the German and Brazilian governments, Halo had a subsidiary, Eden Energy Solutions, begin working on specialised uses for the new energy. Although they faced some opposition from companies and governments that relied heavily on coal or oil, in 1987, a team at Eden led by Alice Sousa discovered the potential for Otherplanar energy to create a small wormhole when focused with a large degree of accuracy. This immediately caught the attention of the managers of the company, and became the primary focus of Eden's research. Sousa's team developed a device that could link two instances of itself with a stable wormhole. Halo immediately patented the technology.

Using the [[Sousa Wormhole Device|Sousa wormhole device]], Halo began development of the Flash-Line in 1988, a network of trains that could instantly move from one station to another via wormholes. It was at this point that the range limitations of the Sousa device were discovered, necessitating intermediate stops for long-distance jumps. An island around the centre of the Atlantic was selected for a central node, and construction began on the Central Atlantic Transport Hub. The Hub would later become the New Atlantian Republic, which Halo would relocate its headquarters to shortly after its declaration of independence.

In 2000, Richardson retired as co-owner of the company, and would be replaced by Paris Charles Harrison, the former Chief Financial Officer. Under Harrison's control, many of the company's assets and systems were streamlined and reworked to provide greater profits, providing Halo with even more resources. In 2005, Sylvester would pass away from heart complications, leaving Harrison as the sole owner. Public opinion of Harrison is significantly more negative than that of the previous owners of the company, and he is often referred to as the Rat King by the New Atlantian public.

Its capture of a titan and construction of an Otherplane engine in 1999 make Halo the only private company to own one, giving it access to resources no other corporation has. Some anti-corporate movements have attempted to protest against private ownership, but Halo has refused all demands to give up control.

## Controversies

A number of controversies surrounding Halo have occurred, with most happening after the change in leadership in the early 2000s.

Halo has always had close ties with the New Atlantian government, but some have criticised its willingness to use its connections in service of its own purposes. Halo has consistently lobbied the New Atlantian government for decreased taxes on corporations and more favourable policies. Halo has also faced some backlash for its internal policies on employees, especially those surrounding its treatment of grunt workers. While the company has taken steps to remedy this, some still argue that the company can afford to do more for its workers.

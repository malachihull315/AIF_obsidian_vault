The Otherplane, also known as the Immaterial Plane or the "Blitzwelt", is a space adjacent to the universe, separated from the universe by an immaterial "barrier". It is composed almost purely of energy, the only examples of material observed within it being the pale titans.

The Otherplane is almost completely inaccessible to anything from the material plane, as the background energy is sufficient to destroy nearly any material objects within seconds. While the Otherplane is one of the most researched phenomena in human history, many of its workings remain unknown. A scientist who researches phenomena related to the Otherplane is a blitzologist. 

## Environment

The Otherplane is a dimension of seemingly limitless energy, taking a semi-fluid form. This fluid state enables the vast amount of energy inside to flow, acting almost like a large body of liquid. Breaks in the barrier between the Otherplane and the material plane cause the flow of energy toward that point, pulling with it the [[Pale Titan|pale titans]] that live there. As titans have been seen on no other planets, the existence of intelligent life is theorised to draw them in.

## Uses in Energy Generation

Otherplanar energy is, in the modern day, the leading source of energy generation in the world. This is done through the [[Otherplane Engine|Otherplane engine]], using a Tear created by a captive titan to extract the fluid energy and convert it to electricity, where it can then be used. Otherplane engines contribute over 90% of the world's electricity, and their existence has vastly reduced the cost of energy, and was the leading contributor to the phasing out of fossil fuel use.

New Atlantis, officially the New Atlantic Republic, formerly known as the Central Atlantic Transport Hub and also colloquially referred to as the Ugly Duckling, is an island country and city located on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge. Due to its remote nature, it does not share any traditional borders with other nations. The nation's territory is composed of the main city and island, and roughly 15,000 square kilometres of ocean.

The city initially existed as an industrial complex during the expansion of the [[Halo Transport Corporation]], becoming the central node for the Halo [[Flash-Line]] network in 1991. As the network expanded, so too did the traffic to the city, causing a rapid boom in population. This quick growth necessitated the construction of large districts of high-density housing.

New Atlantis is one of the world's newest recognised nations, declaring independence from its parent country of Brazil in 2003, after the surge in tourism and trade in the wake of the September 11th attacks.

New Atlantis is one of the most highly developed countries in the world, possessing a very high GDP per capita, and one of the highest employment rates of all countries. Its population density is also very high, and it is consistently ranked in the top 30 most liveable cities. New Atlantis is a highly diverse cultural "melting pot", due to its population's varied backgrounds and the city's status as the centre of the world's biggest transport and trade networks. Its main language is English, but a significant portion of its population also speaks Spanish or Portuguese, due to the country's close ties with the Americas.

New Atlantis was established as a republic by its leaders upon the declaration of independence, and is a multi-party democracy based on its constitution. Despite this, however, many have argued that its government's relationship with Halo is reminiscent of more oligarchical societies.

It is a member of NATO and the United Nations, and is an ally of many countries across the world due to its unrivalled access to [[The Otherplane|Otherplane]]-based energy, and housing the Halo Flash-Line.

## History

The Central Atlantic Transport Hub started construction in 1988, shortly after the beginning of the construction for Halo's [[Flash-Line]]. It was selected due to its being a halfway point between the American and European landmasses. This allows portals created here to reach land on both sides of the Atlantic Ocean, where stations could much more easily be built, rather than relying on floating infrastructure or more ocean-based construction. The Transport Hub was completed in late 1991, when the Flash-Line first became active.

While expansion did occur throughout the 1990s, the Flash-Line was much less prevalent than in the modern day. Without an [[Otherplane Engine|Otherplane engine]] located in the Hub, it was much more difficult to run wormhole devices, and the Flash-Line remained somewhat of a luxury, only realistically available to large corporations or the extremely wealthy. The rapid growth of the Hub, and the beginning of its transformation into the city of New Atlantis, started in 2001. In the wake of the September 11th attacks, the Flash-Line was turned to by many governments and companies as a safer alternative to planes for transport and shipping. In addition to this, the capture of a pale titan by Halo in 2000 allowed for massive upscaling of the rail network.

This increased traffic led to Halo's profits increasing by a large margin, and necessitated the expansion of the Flash-Line network. The number of workers and resources required for this led to the construction of accommodation and utilities on-site, leading to the Transport Hub's growth into a city. Eventually, the Flash-Line would expand to a global scale, and New Atlantis would steadily grow into the major power that it is today.

In 2012, a titanbreak would occur over the Atlantic Ocean, and the [[New Atlantis Centre for Otherplane Research and Hazard Prevention]] would manage to successfully restrain the pale titan. It was then moved onto a modified aircraft carrier for the purpose of transporting it to the main city. Frankie, as it would come to be called, was moved slowly into an Otherplane engine located on the seabed. After this, the electricity generation in New Atlantis skyrocketed, allowing for the city to expand even further.

## Politics and Economy

New Atlantis economically relies heavily on tourism, due to possessing the centrepiece of most of the planet's transportation network.

New Atlantis' prime exports are electricity, due to its two [[Otherplane Engine|Otherplane engines]] (the highest number of any one city, and tied with China for the highest number possessed by one country), and technology suited to use with Otherplanar fluid energy (OPFE). Its large industrial strength for its size coupled with its leading technology for Otherplane use renders it a capable military power, more than able to defend itself from outside threats.

New Atlantis' leader is the President, for which an election is held every 6 years.

The Halo Transport Corporation continues to operate out of New Atlantis, with its main headquarters being based there. Several have criticised the close ties the company has with the government, but the New Atlantis government and Halo remain on good terms.

As New Atlantis was owned by a Brazilian company, the land was considered a remote territory of Brazil until its declaration of independence. As the island itself is situated at ~40° north, ~35° west, it was offered an invitation to NATO in 2007.
